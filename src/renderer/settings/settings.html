  1. Create and customize a humanoid avatar at Avaturn.
  2. Export a T-pose GLB with its skeleton and embedded textures (up to 25 MB).

Kai is included. Imported avatars stay on this computer. Avaturn is a separate service with its own terms and privacy policy. Supported rigs use standard Avaturn bone names; other models may need adjustments.
